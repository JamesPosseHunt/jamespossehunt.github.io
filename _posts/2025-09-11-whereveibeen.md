# Where've I Been
Hey, I know I went quiet for months. I didn't make much progress on the site or anything of the sort because of my visa.

In Japan, if you're here on a work visa, you have 3 months to find work. Over the last couple months, I was looking for a new job. I found one eventually, working for the sound people at World Family English (in Nagoya and Okazaki).

So my time over the last time was split between the job hunt, a slump when I realized I couldn't visit home as I'd probably not be let back into the country without a job if I left, and adjusting to the new job.

I'll say now that it did seem a pain in the ass. When I started I was sitting in on classes - then it got worse. I started having to teach my own lessons while learning their methods and write up lesson plans on my own time.

Now though, things are getting better. The schedule's really good, I'm figuring out how to manage my time so I'm left time during the week to get these plans done, and I foresee becoming a lot more productive.

So yeah, that's where I was. My next update should be on time next week.
